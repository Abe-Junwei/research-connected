# Research Connected

Canonical user-facing terms for literature discovery and reading.

## Surfaces

**Graph / 图谱**:
The similarity map. A note embed and the workbench both display this graph.
_Avoid_: using 图谱 as the name of the wide pane.

**Workbench / 工作台**:
The wider pane for projects, frequently cited references, works citing this graph, the research narrative, and export.
_Avoid_: 图谱 for this pane. Research Connected is the product name, not the name of the pane.

## Literature and evidence

**Seed paper / 种子文献**:
The starting work used to build a literature graph. It remains visible when other works are filtered.
_Avoid_: 起点论文, 中心论文, 种子论文

**Frequently cited references / 图内常引文献**:
Works cited by at least two currently visible papers. Inclusion does not establish chronological priority or intellectual influence.
_Avoid_: 先验工作, Prior works

**Works citing this graph / 引用本图的文献**:
Works whose available reference lists include at least two currently visible papers. Citation does not establish intellectual inheritance.
_Avoid_: 衍生工作, Derivative works, Later work

**Citation–recency score / 被引与时效分**:
A ranking signal based on citation counts and publication recency, not a measure of scholarly authority or research quality.
_Avoid_: 权威分, Authority score

**Selection score / 入选排序分**:
A score used to rank eligible candidates, distinct from the completed graph's similarity scores. It is not a probability of relevance.
_Avoid_: 选择时相关性, Relevance at selection

**Semantic similarity / 语义相似度**:
Similarity inferred from available text, topics, or compatible semantic vectors. It is not direct citation evidence.
_Avoid_: 文本相似度 when vectors or topics contribute

**High-citation indicator / 高被引提示**:
A heuristic citation-based indicator, not certification that a paper is a scholarly classic.
_Avoid_: 经典文献, Classic paper

**Citation clusters / 引文群组**:
Algorithmic groups in the sampled citation and similarity graph, not established schools of thought.
_Avoid_: 引用团

## Reading and projects

**Reading status / 阅读状态**:
One of unread, to read, or read, shared across projects and confirmed versions.
_Avoid_: 阅读进度, Reading progress

**Save paper / 收藏文献**:
Keep a paper in the current project's saved list, independently of its reading status.
_Avoid_: 暂存论文, Stage paper

**Save project / 保存项目**:
Preserve the project's graph and current view. This is separate from saving an individual paper.
_Avoid_: 保存视图 when referring to the project-save action

**Note properties / 笔记属性（YAML frontmatter）**:
Structured metadata at the beginning of a Markdown note, distinct from its prose.
_Avoid_: 前言
